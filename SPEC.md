9. AI FOOD PHOTO SCANNER

Large button "SCAN MY MEAL". Flow: 1) Take a photo with phone camera. 2) Upload existing photo. 3) Submit for AI analysis. 4) Review identified foods and estimated portions. 5) Correct anything inaccurate. 6) Confirm and save.
Use a real multimodal vision model through a secure backend integration. Identify: food items, approximate portion sizes, likely preparation methods, estimated calories, protein, carbs, fats.
Display a clearly labelled per-item table (Food / Calories / Protein ...) plus meal total — values from the actual analysis, not hardcoded.
Accuracy: AI must acknowledge exact weights, oils, sauces, ingredients and cooking methods cannot always be determined from a photo. Display an uncertainty indicator and allow manual corrections. If uncertainty is high, ask a useful follow-up question (e.g., "Was the chicken fried or grilled?", "Approximately how much rice did you have?", "Was the sauce creamy or tomato-based?"). Allow adjusting amounts and automatically recalculate macros.
Large "CONFIRM & ADD TO DAILY NUTRITION" button. Only after confirmation is the meal saved. Must immediately update daily calories, protein, carbs, fats, dashboard rings and meal history. Allow edits and undoing accidental entries.

10. AI FOOD RESEARCH CHAT — "ASK FOOD COACH"

Describe what I ate in normal language; AI interprets, identifies items, estimates or retrieves nutrition, calculates totals. Examples: "three eggs, two bagels, and a protein shake"; "250 grams of cooked chicken and two cups of rice"; "a medium Domino's pepperoni pizza and eight boneless chicken pieces"; "a large bowl of homemade bolognese pasta".
Restaurant nutrition research (extremely important): for branded foods or restaurant orders, look for actual published nutrition information where accessible. Source preference: 1) Official restaurant nutrition info 2) Manufacturer labels 3) Reputable databases such as USDA FoodData Central 4) Other identifiable nutrition databases 5) Clearly labelled estimates if trustworthy exact data cannot be found. For chains, account for country, serving size, crust type, toppings, sauces, quantity. Whenever live research is supported, show the source name and link. Never invent a citation, claim to have researched without actually retrieving data, or present guessed restaurant macros as verified facts.
Conversational follow-ups: e.g., "I had pizza and wings" → AI asks restaurant, size, wing count → user answers → breakdown → "Actually, I only ate six slices" → AI updates total → "Add it to my food log" → confirmation summary and save.
Chat must support: Real conversation history; Meal context across follow-ups; Food search; Portion corrections; Meal substitutions; Nutrition explanations; Saved food favourites; One-click add to daily totals; Optional photo attachment; Clearly displayed source/estimate labels.
